Visiting Kyrgyzstan: Independent Travel or a Group Tour?

2022/03/252 min read

"Can you travel Kyrgyzstan independently?" It's probably the question we're asked most. The answer: yes, but you need to know what you'll face first.

Ask Ruby, a seasoned backpacker who has traveled independently through more than 30 countries, and she'll say:

"If I were young and full of energy, with plenty of time, happy to use sign language and a translation app, on a small budget, patient enough to bargain, and ready to face all kinds of unknowns, then of course I could travel Kyrgyzstan on my own!"

But the reality is that Kyrgyzstan isn't an easy place for first-time independent travelers. Compared with more popular destinations, there are a few key things you need to consider first.

Think about these four things before going it alone

1. Language: English barely works here 🗣

Kyrgyzstan's official languages are Kyrgyz and Russian. Even in the capital, Bishkek, few people speak English; in remote areas you'll mostly rely on body language and translation apps. The problem is that these alone rarely solve the real challenges of travel: booking cars, finding places to stay, checking routes…

2. Roads: head into the mountains and the asphalt disappears 🚗

Apart from a few main roads running north to south, as soon as you head into the mountains it's mostly gravel, dirt tracks, or no road at all. Sights are far apart, routes are complicated, and GPS isn't always right. Without local experience, driving is exhausting; a professional driver makes the trip far less stressful.

3. Hiking safety: mountain weather changes in a moment ⛰

Many people come for the spectacular mountains and hiking, but the terrain is high, the weather changeable and temperatures swing widely, so it's easy to get into danger without experience. A guide doesn't just make you safer; you also get to learn about local culture. Two benefits in one.

4. Little information: a dozen ways from A to B 🏕

This is definitely the biggest challenge of independent travel. There may be a dozen ways to get from A to B, but every local gives you different information, and with the language barrier, just working out which transport to take can take hours. Traveling solo is harder; going independently with friends or joining a small group is a better choice.

So which one suits you?

✔Independent travel: for you if you love a challenge, aren't put off by language barriers, have plenty of time and are happy to figure things out slowly.

✔A local guide or a group tour: for you if you'd like to explore more easily and efficiently, and save your time for the landscapes and the people.

Whichever way you choose, Kyrgyzstan's landscapes and culture are more than worth a deep, unhurried journey.
