The Best Ride of My Life in Kyrgyzstan, the "Switzerland of Central Asia"

2024/06/142 min read

We left Bishkek at 5:30 in the morning and drove for a full 12 hours, arriving only in the evening. The next morning I opened the window and found it had snowed in the night. That day became the best ride of my life.

The deeper into the mountains, the clearer the trip becomes

We stayed one night by Kel-Suu (Köl-Suu) Lake, ready to ride to the lake the next day. Here we were already above 3,000 m.

The moment the asphalt ended and the car turned onto a gravel track, I could feel what kind of trip this would be. Another reminder: there were no modern toilets where we stayed that night. A little wooden hut standing on the steppe was the outhouse.

I opened the window and the snowy peaks were right there. A simple nomad cabin, a simple dinner: I understood all of it. This is the way of traveling closest to nature.

Setting off in May snow

We woke up to find that May snow had fallen overnight. We would ride to the lake through falling snow. The lake sits above the snow line, still holding snow that hadn't melted all winter.

The trail is less than ten kilometers and could be hiked, but in snow, riding is better: horses easily cross mud and streams that are hard going on foot.

Snowy peaks, yaks, and the clip-clop of hooves

Riding among snowy mountains at 3,000 m is a unique, unforgettable experience. You breathe fresh mountain air and listen to the crisp sound of hooves on earth and rock, while snow-covered peaks sparkle in the sun as if you were in a film.

At moments like this, a horse isn't just transport; it's a way of being close to nature. The views are wide open: yaks in the valley below, and snowy ridges rolling into the distance.

A horse that wants to run, and a nervous me

At this altitude the weather changes in an instant: bright sun one moment, snow the next. We rode across streams and up steep slopes, and whenever the horses reached flat snowy ground, they got excited and wanted to run.

That made me nervous: I've fallen off a horse twice before, so when a horse speeds up, I get a little scared. The teenage boy riding next to me wasn't scared at all. He squeezed his horse's sides, hoping it would go faster.

Three hours later, I'd learned

On this ride we spent a full 3 hours among snowy peaks and herds of yaks, riding between the nomads' camp and a mountain lake formed by a landslide. By the end, I had finally learned a little about riding at a gallop.

After the ride, a cup of hot tea or coffee brings a warmth that reaches your bones: another kind of pleasure words can't describe.

Want to go to Kel-Suu? The lake lies in a restricted zone near the Chinese border, so you need a border permit in advance. With the long journey and changeable weather, we recommend going with an experienced local team.

Riding in Kyrgyzstan has been my most beautiful experience.
