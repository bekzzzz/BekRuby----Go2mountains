How to choose

Private tour or group tour?

Private tour

From 6 people you have your own group, at your own pace

Who it's for

  • You already have 6 or more friends or family coming
  • Family trips with grandparents or children: we can adjust the itinerary to your needs
  • Travelers with special wishes for stays or plans, or who value privacy

Group tour

Better value, and new travel friends from around the world

Who it's for

  • 1–2 travelers, or a group of friends smaller than 6
  • You want a friendlier budget and the best value

Departs with 10 people, max 12: a small group, never crowded

Seasons at a Glance

Kyrgyzstan Through the Year

Pick a season to see what it feels like in the mountains, valleys, and lakes.

March – May

Spring

The countryside comes alive with wildflowers, poppy fields, and fresh greenery, perfect for low-elevation hikes and enjoying nature's awakening.

June – August

Summer

It's the perfect season for outdoor adventures: trek open mountain trails, visit sunlit alpine lakes like Issyk-Kul, and stay in yurt camps for a nomadic experience.

September – November

Autumn

Golden and crimson landscapes, cooler weather, and stunning valleys make it ideal for exploring and photography.

December – February

Winter

A snowy wonderland awaits with skiing, snowboarding, hot springs, and peaceful mountain serenity.
